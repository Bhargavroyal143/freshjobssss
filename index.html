FAQs

Is FreshJobs free?

Yes. Browsing jobs, internships and using career tools is free for students and freshers. We never ask candidates for money for a job.

Is FreshJobs the employer?

No. We share openings from companies. You apply directly on the company's own page using the Apply button.

Why do I need an account?

Jobs and internships are open to everyone. Career tools, notes and resume tools need a free account so your progress is saved.

Is my resume stored anywhere?

No. The resume checker reads your file in your browser. Resume builder drafts stay on your own device.

An apply link is not working. What should I do?

The opening may have closed. Search the role on the company's careers page, or tell us through Feedback so we can update it.

How do I spot fake job offers?
  • Real companies never ask for money for training, registration or an offer letter.
  • Check that emails come from the company's official domain.
  • Be careful of offers made without any interview.
  • Never share OTPs or bank details during hiring.
